How a DM becomes a quote

Someone messages a photography business on Instagram. Seconds later they have a price. This is every step in between — and the one rule that keeps the AI from inventing your prices.

The rule everything else follows

Gemini understands

Reads the message and reports what it means. That is all it is allowed to do.

Python decides

Checks that meaning against the approved list, then decides what changes. Nothing else may touch the conversation.

Sheets is the truth

Every service, package and price comes from the business’s own spreadsheet. Edit it and the bot changes.
